Our Story

History of Gonzaga

A short journey through the story and development of our hometown.

Early Communities

The area that is now Gonzaga was inhabited by communities whose lives were closely connected with the land, forests, rivers and coastal environment.

1918 — Gonzaga Becomes a Municipality

Gonzaga was established as an independent municipality in 1918. The municipality was named in honor of Gracio P. Gonzaga, who served as the first Filipino governor of Cagayan.

Growth of Agriculture and Coastal Communities

Agriculture, fishing, coconut production and other local livelihoods became important parts of Gonzaga's development.

Gonzaga Today

Modern Gonzaga continues to develop while its communities work to preserve its natural resources, local traditions, agriculture and cultural identity.
